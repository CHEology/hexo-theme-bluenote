A page can leave enough room for a thought without making the reader cross an empty screen. The text stays in one column, while the surrounding space remains quiet.

纸张的颜色、文字的疏密和段落之间的停顿，共同构成阅读的节奏。中文、English 和数字 123 在这里使用相同的字号；换一种语言，不需要换一套版面。

## One reading column

This article contains three illustrations. It remains a normal article: adding a picture does not turn the surrounding prose into a photography layout.

![A blue rectangle within a warm field.](/images/study-1-600.png)

A caption belongs to the illustration when the author supplies it. It should remain visible, just as headings and the table of contents remain available.

## 两种语言，一段文字

有人习惯从头读到尾，也有人在几个段落之间来回。清楚的层级让两种读法都能成立。我们保留原来的段落，不为页面的装饰改写它们。

![Two quiet vertical forms.](/images/study-2-600.png)

> A quotation has its own shape, but shares the same reading space.

The ordinary details also need to work:

- Links stay easy to identify.
- Lists use the same text measure.
- Code can scroll without widening the page.

```js
const page = { words: 'first', images: 'when needed' };
console.log(page);
```
